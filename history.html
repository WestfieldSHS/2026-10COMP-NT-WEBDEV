History of Vietnam

Ancient Dynasties

Vietnam’s history dates back thousands of years, beginning with the legendary Hong Bang dynasty and later powerful dynasties like the Ly, Tran, and Le.

French Colonization

In the 19th century, Vietnam became part of French Indochina, influencing architecture, cuisine, and education.

Vietnam War

The Vietnam War (1955–1975) was a major conflict that shaped modern Vietnam. The country reunified in 1975.
